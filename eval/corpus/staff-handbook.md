# Staff Handbook

## Staff arrival and duty

Staff sign in at the staff room by 07:15. Each teacher does playground duty one break a week, according to the duty roster on the staff room notice board.

## Leave applications

Staff apply for leave on form L2, which must reach the principal at least 10 school days before the leave starts. Sick leave is reported to the deputy principal by 06:30 on the day.

## Staff Wi-Fi

The staff Wi-Fi network is KPS-Staff. The password changes every term, and this term it is Heron-2027-Kites. Do not share the password with learners or parents.

## Emergency lockdown

If the principal announces the code word BLUE KESTREL over the intercom, the school is in lockdown. Lock the classroom door, keep learners away from the windows, and wait for the all-clear.

## Learner disciplinary hearings

Disciplinary hearings for learners are chaired by the deputy principal. The learner's parents are invited at least five school days before the hearing.
