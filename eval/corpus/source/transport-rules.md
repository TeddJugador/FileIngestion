# Transport Rules

## Routes and times

The school runs two bus routes. Route A leaves the Hillcrest stop at 06:50 and Route B leaves the Seaview stop at 06:55. Both buses arrive at school by 07:30. In the afternoon, both buses leave the school at 14:10.

## Behaviour on the bus

Learners stay seated with their seatbelts fastened for the whole journey. Eating and drinking on the bus are not allowed. A learner who misbehaves on the bus is suspended from the bus service for one week, and the parents are informed in writing.

## Transport fees

The bus service costs R650 per learner per month, paid together with the school fees. Parents give one month's notice in writing to stop using the bus.

## Aftercare learners

Learners who attend aftercare do not travel on the afternoon bus. Parents collect them from aftercare by 17:30.
