# Uniform Policy

## Summer uniform

The summer uniform, worn in terms 1 and 4, is a white short-sleeved golf shirt with the school crest, navy shorts or a navy skirt, and white ankle socks.

## Winter uniform

The winter uniform, worn in terms 2 and 3, is a white long-sleeved shirt, the school tie, a navy jersey, and grey long trousers or a navy tunic with navy tights.

## Shoes

Learners wear black leather lace-up school shoes. Trainers are only allowed for physical education lessons and sport.

## Hair and jewellery

Hair longer than the collar must be tied back. Hair accessories must be navy or white. The only jewellery allowed is a watch and one pair of plain stud earrings.

## Physical education kit

For physical education, learners wear their house T-shirt, navy shorts and white trainers. The kit is brought to school in a separate bag on PE days.

## Second-hand uniform shop

The second-hand uniform shop in the school hall is open on Wednesdays from 07:15 to 08:00. Parents may also donate outgrown uniform items there.
