# Assessment Policy

## Assessment calendar

Formal tests are written in weeks 6 and 9 of each term. The test timetable is sent to parents two weeks before each test week.

## Missed assessments

A learner who misses a formal test must bring a medical certificate or a letter from the parent to the class teacher within three school days. The learner then writes a catch-up test. Without a valid reason, a missed test receives a mark of zero.

## Homework

Learners in Grades 1 to 3 receive about 20 minutes of homework each school day. Learners in Grades 4 to 7 receive about 45 minutes. No homework is given on Fridays.

## Reports

Report cards are issued on the last Friday of each term. Parents may book a meeting with the class teacher to discuss the report.

## Promotion requirements

To be promoted to the next grade, a learner must pass Home Language with at least 50 percent and Mathematics with at least 40 percent.
