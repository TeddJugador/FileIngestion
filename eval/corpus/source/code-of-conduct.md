# Code of Conduct

## Arrival and punctuality

The school gates open at 07:00 and lessons begin at 07:45. A learner who arrives after 07:50 is late and must sign the late register at the front office before going to class. A learner who is late three times in one term serves a Friday detention from 13:30 to 14:30, and the class teacher informs the parents in writing.

## Respect and behaviour

Learners treat every person at school with courtesy. They greet visitors, wait their turn, and look after school property. Running in the corridors is not allowed, and learners walk on the left of the stairs.

## Cellphones

Learners may bring a cellphone for safety reasons, but it must be switched off and handed to the class teacher at 07:45. Phones are returned at 13:45. A phone used during the school day is confiscated, and only a parent may collect it from the front office.

## Bullying

Bullying in any form, including online bullying, is a serious offence. Learners or parents should report bullying to the grade head within 24 hours. The grade head meets everyone involved within three school days and records what was agreed.

## Merits and demerits

Good conduct earns merits, and breaking these rules earns demerits. Five demerits in a term lead to a letter to the parents. Ten demerits in a term lead to a meeting with the principal, and further steps may follow under the disciplinary procedure.
