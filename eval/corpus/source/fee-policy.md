# Fee Policy 2027

## Annual school fees

Annual school fees for 2027 depend on the learner's grade.

| Grade | Annual fee | Monthly instalment |
|---|---|---|
| Grade R | R18 600 | R1 860 |
| Grades 1 to 3 | R24 000 | R2 400 |
| Grades 4 to 7 | R27 600 | R2 760 |

## Paying fees

Parents may pay the annual fee in ten monthly instalments from February to November, due on the 7th of each month. Parents who pay the full annual fee by 31 January receive a discount of 5 percent. Payments are made by EFT, using the learner's admission number as the reference.

## Late payment

Interest of 1.5 percent per month is charged on any amount that is more than 30 days overdue. Parents who are struggling to pay should contact the bursar before the due date to arrange a payment plan.

## Sibling discount

The second child from the same family enrolled at the school receives a 10 percent discount on fees. The third and any further children receive a 20 percent discount each.

## Fee exemptions

Parents who cannot afford the fees may apply for a full or partial exemption. Applications are made on form F1 to the fees committee by 31 March, with proof of income.
